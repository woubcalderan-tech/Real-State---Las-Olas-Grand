Privacy Policy

This notice explains how the Las Olas Grand Unit 2010 website uses optional Google Analytics.

Website responsibilities and privacy contact

Solange, the property owner, is responsible for processing website visitor data.

The website developer maintains the site, manages Google Analytics, and handles technical privacy requests on Solange’s behalf. PD Realty is the property’s listing brokerage.

For privacy questions or requests, email Solange at ds.solange@gmail.com.

Your choice

Google Analytics is off by default and loads only after you select Allow analytics in Analytics preferences in the footer. You can browse the listing, view photos, and use the contact links with analytics off.

If you opt in, we use analytics to understand how visitors use the listing and which pages and contact options they find useful.

What analytics measures

Google Analytics can measure visits, page views, pages visited, and the page language. It can also receive browser and device information, approximate location inferred from your IP address, and identifiers stored in analytics cookies that help distinguish browsers and sessions.

Our custom events record clicks on phone, email, and brokerage website links, and on links to showing or contact information. They record the page path and language and, where applicable, the contact role, method, or type of link. The custom events exclude names, phone numbers, email addresses, and message contents.

A click indicates interest. It does not confirm that a call connected, an email was sent, or an appointment was booked or completed.

How Google is involved

When you consent, this site sends analytics information to Google, which processes it to provide Google Analytics reports. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Change or withdraw consent

Open Analytics preferences in the footer and select Decline analytics to stop future analytics collection through this site in this browser. Select Allow analytics if you choose to opt in again. The site saves your choice in this browser when local storage is available.

When you withdraw consent, the site attempts to remove its Google Analytics cookies that it can access in this browser. This does not delete information previously collected by Google Analytics. You can clear any remaining cookies in your browser settings. Clearing all site data can also reset your saved preference. Analytics will then remain off until you opt in again.
