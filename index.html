Wake up to the Pacific

High on the 20th floor of Las Olas Grand, this secluded end residence has some of the best views in the building and opens almost entirely to the ocean. Floor-to-ceiling sliding glass doors run along the living room and both bedrooms, opening onto a 703 sq ft wraparound terrace with a glass railing and open views up and down the Rosarito coastline.

Inside, the home is bright and finished with care: polished travertine floors, crown moulding, an arched entry door, built-in cabinetry and mirrored wardrobes, a white kitchen with quartz counters and marble backsplash, and a primary bath with a jetted tub, glass shower and hand-laid mosaic details. It comes with a deeded parking space and full use of the building's resort amenities.
